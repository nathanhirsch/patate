The root

Sweet potato is real endurance fuel.

We didn't pick it for the color. A sweet potato carries dense, slow carbohydrate plus the potassium and magnesium you sweat out — which is why it has fed athletes long before it was a bakery ingredient.

01

Carbohydrate that lasts

Mostly complex starch, so it releases over hours instead of spiking and dropping. 40 g a serving — a real ride's worth, not a gel's.

02

Potassium and magnesium

The two minerals you lose fastest in sweat, and the ones cramping legs ask for. A sweet potato has more potassium than a banana.

03

Beta-carotene

The orange is vitamin A. It is the one nutrient in this cake that has nothing to do with performance and everything to do with eating well.

04

Easy at effort

No grain, no gluten, no dairy. Cooked starch and eggs, which is about the gentlest thing you can ask a stomach to handle at mile 20.
